## What's new in v1.2.23

- **Words with a hyphen or an apostrophe now convert.** `rjt-xnj` → `кое-что`, `gj-heccrb` → `по-русски`, `v\zrj` → `мʼяко`. Before, a hyphen or the Ukrainian apostrophe ended the word early, so auto-correct never fired on such words at all.
- **Fixing a typo with Backspace no longer splits the word.** Typing `g\`, deleting a character, then finishing the word used to convert only the part typed after the deletion.
- **Nothing lands in the middle of a fix any more.** Keys pressed while text is being retyped are held and typed back right after — now on the hotkey path too, not only in auto-correct. This is what caused half-converted words and a space turning into the last character typed.
- **With nothing selected, the hotkey converts the word at the caret.** It no longer grabs the whole line and guesses which part was typed in the wrong layout, and it no longer retypes the rest of the line.
