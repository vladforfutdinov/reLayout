## What's new in v1.2.24

Auto-correct fixes:

- **Punctuation right after a word no longer blocks the fix.** A word typed in the wrong layout with a `?`, `!` or similar right after it is now corrected together with the punctuation when you press space: `ltkfq? ` becomes `делай, `. Before, the word was left as typed.
- **Enter no longer auto-corrects.** Enter submits things (a Raycast search, a chat message), and correcting first made Raycast search for the converted text and launch the wrong app. Now Enter sends exactly what you typed. To fix a word before sending, press the hotkey or type a space first.
- **Tab stays a real Tab after a correction.** After a fix it used to arrive as a text character, so forms did not move to the next field.
- **No more stray or doubled words after clicking or shortcuts.** After a click, Cmd+V, Cmd+Z, Cmd+Tab and similar, auto-correct forgets the unfinished word. Before, the next fix could delete text at the new cursor position and paste an earlier word there.
- **Fast typing through a fix no longer scrambles text** when the keys you pressed during the fix held more than one word.
