## What's new in v1.3.0 — macOS

- **The hotkey converts only what you select.** It no longer guesses the word before the caret when nothing is selected — that guess could rewrite text you didn't mean. Select the text (or just the word) and press the hotkey; words you have just typed are what auto-correct is for.
- **Auto-correct stays out of virtual machines and remote desktops.** UTM, Parallels, VMware Fusion, VirtualBox and Microsoft Remote Desktop are now on the exceptions list: the guest system has its own layout, and a correction made from the Mac could type garbage there (e.g. a row of `aaaa` in a Windows VM). They are added once to your existing list; remove any of them to turn auto-correct back on there.
- **The Exceptions sheet explains itself** — a line under the title says what the list does, and an ⓘ tells why terminals, editors and VMs are listed from the start.
- **Undo is safer:** a click after a conversion ends the press-again undo window, so a late second press can't replace text at the new caret position.
