## What's new in v1.3.11 — Windows (preview)

- Three or more layouts: press the hotkey again within ~1.5 s to retype the original text into the next layout, and once every layout has had its turn, to undo.
- After an undo, the hotkey keeps working on the same text.
- Polish and Portuguese language models for auto-correct.
- Two quick hotkey presses with nothing selected no longer trigger DeepL.
