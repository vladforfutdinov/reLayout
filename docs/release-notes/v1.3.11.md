## What's new in v1.3.11 — macOS

- Three or more layouts: press the hotkey again within ~1.5 s to retype the original text into the next layout, and once every layout has had its turn, to undo. With two layouts the second press is still the undo.
- After an undo, the hotkey keeps working on the same text — before, it had nothing to act on until you typed or selected again.
- With US and Polish (or another pair of layouts that share every key) plus a Cyrillic one, the hotkey converted nothing from the Latin layouts; it now goes to the Cyrillic one.
- Polish and Portuguese language models: auto-correct now works on those layouts next to a Cyrillic one.
- The doubled first letter after a Globe switch (a macOS quirk) no longer makes auto-correct eat the space before the word.
- In apps without an Accessibility selection (Electron, Claude), a copied image or file survives the hotkey's clipboard round-trip, and two quick presses with nothing selected no longer trigger DeepL.
