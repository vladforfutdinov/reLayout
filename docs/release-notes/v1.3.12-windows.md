## What's new in v1.3.12 — Windows (preview)

- Russian typed on the Ukrainian layout is no longer turned into English junk ("ещё" → "tot"); a word counts as junk only if none of your Cyrillic layouts reads it.
- Auto-correct catches more words with letters on punctuation keys, and a short word it cannot judge no longer breaks a chain of wrong-layout words.
- Learned words editor: candidates and confirmed verdicts with checkboxes, "+" / "−", "Clean candidates" and "Reset" (both confirmed); a verdict needs three hotkey corrections. Exceptions has "+" / "−" instead of Choose… and Remove.
