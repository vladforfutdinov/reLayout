## What's new in v1.3.12 — macOS

- Russian typed on the Ukrainian layout is safe again. After the 1.3.10 Ukrainian model rebuild, a Russian word like "ещё" looked like junk to it, and its English reading "tot" is a real word, so auto-correct turned "ещё" into "tot". Now a word counts as junk only if none of your Cyrillic layouts reads it.
- Auto-correct catches more: words with letters that sit on punctuation keys (х, ж, э, є, ї, б, ю) were held to a stricter bar that rejected one in seven real words ("халепа"); and a short word auto-correct cannot judge ("ot" in "nf ot [fktgf") no longer breaks a chain of wrong-layout words — it is fixed along with them.
- Learned words got a proper editor. A word becomes a candidate on the first hotkey correction and a confirmed verdict on the third; the window lists both, with a checkbox to confirm or demote. Add your own with "+" (leave "Becomes" empty to keep a word as typed), remove with "−", clear candidates or reset everything — both ask first. Single letters and deliberate transliterations of correct text are no longer learned.
- The Exceptions and Learned words windows are real windows with a close button and the Login Items look; Exceptions has "+" / "−" instead of Choose… and the hover remove button.
- Updating from 1.3.8 and later works again (the update feed was signed with the old key); installs older than 1.3.8 are shown the download page instead of an error.
