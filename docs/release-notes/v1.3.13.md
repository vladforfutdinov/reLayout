## What's new in v1.3.13 — macOS

- Russian typed on the Ukrainian layout stays Russian even without a Russian layout installed: "еще" no longer turns into "tot". Likewise Ukrainian on the Russian layout ("якщо" no longer becomes "zroj") and English on a German, French, Spanish, Polish or Portuguese layout ("but" no longer becomes "иге").
- A word that is junk in both layouts is left alone instead of being converted into different junk.
- Three-letter words that are real in both layouts — "рук" and "her" are the same keys — are no longer fixed on their own: "сколько нам нужно рук" stays, while "рук ыщт" still becomes "her son" together with its neighbour.
- Russian typed for the Ukrainian layout with letters on punctuation keys is caught: "c,jhrf" becomes "сборка".
- "Between similar layouts" can be turned on only when you have two layouts of one script in different languages (e.g. Ukrainian and Russian); its ⓘ says so.
